# Retake Coach: public demo

Retake Coach checks a fundus photo the moment it is taken: keep or retake, and what to fix (focus, aim or
something in the way), with the problem area marked on the photo. It checks photo quality only and gives no medical
advice.

Click a sample photo on the page, or try your own test images. **Please don't upload patient photos:** this demo runs on
a shared server. In a clinic, Retake Coach runs on the computer next to the camera, and photos stay there.

Code, results and limits: [github.com/roninram/retake-coach](https://github.com/roninram/retake-coach).
Sample photos: DeepDRiD (CC BY-SA 4.0) and scikit-image.
